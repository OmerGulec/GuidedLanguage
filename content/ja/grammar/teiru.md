ている is the て form of a verb followed by いる, "to be there". It says that what the verb names is in place at the time spoken of: either the action is still going on, or the change it names has happened and its result is still there.

The verb decides which. An action that takes time, such as 読む or 待つ, is in progress: 本を読んでいる, is reading a book. A change that is over in a moment, such as 壊れる or 来る, has happened and its result holds: 壊れている, is broken; 来ている, has come and is here.

ている conjugates as いる does: ていない, not; ていた, past; ています, polite. In speech いる often loses its い: 読んでる, 起きてる.
