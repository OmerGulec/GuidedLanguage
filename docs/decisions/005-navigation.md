# 005 — Navigation

Status: accepted

Four places: **Today · Library · Dictionary · You.** The interface never shows internal terms like facet or card.

**Today.** The only guide. Shows its parts, e.g. "Reviews 84 · New 8 · Reading 2"; each starts on its own, Continue runs them all. New cards stop at the daily new budget, after which Continue goes to reading. There is a clear end.

**Library.** Works like autoplay: the app picks the passage that fits you best, and every passage shows its context (source, position, author or site, genre) so you can go there. One player with a text toggle: shown, or hidden until the end. Dictation is an exercise Today schedules. Early material is single sentences, growing to paragraphs as enough becomes available.

**Dictionary.** Search first, then Words / Grammar / Kanji with your status on every row. Every item has exactly one page, linked from everywhere; a grammar point's page is its lesson. The grammar curriculum is an ordered list with prerequisites. Kanji ranking and "often missed" are sorts and filters here.

**You.** Coverage, history, goals, import and export, settings.

**First run.** Beginners start with kana. Importers start with import: resolve ambiguous matches, map their card types to our cards, take a short grammar placement, then see what their words already let them read and what to learn next.
