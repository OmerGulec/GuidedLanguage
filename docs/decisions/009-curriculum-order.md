# 009 — Curriculum order

Status: accepted

**One queue.** Words, set phrases and grammar uses not yet introduced or marked, plus rungs that have just unlocked, compete for the daily new budget. Each is ranked by how often its item appears in the library (once per passage, each source's share capped; a grammar use by its highest probability in the passage), divided by the effort for its item kind. A smarter ranking replaces this only after beating it in the simulator (012). Ties break by id.

**Kanji** are ranked separately: by how many of the learner's known words contain them, weighted by how often those words are missed.

**Hard edges.** Kana first. A form after what it is derived from (007). A pattern after its parts, unless met as a chunk. A system lesson before its members. A formation before the uses built on it.

**Building blocks** (formations, system lessons) rank with the cheapest item that depends on them.

**Caps.** Re-teaching comes before anything new. Grammar uses and kanji have their own daily caps within the new budget.

**The graph.** Grammar items, their parts and edges are authored data (010), seeded from a hand-ordered beginner list with vocabulary moved to words and the grammar natural text needs added: てしまう, ようだ/みたい/らしい, はず, わけ, ため, ように, ところ, ばかり, honorific and humble verbs, and the structure items of 008.

**Placement.** Systems and structure items can be passed with a quick check, so learners whose first language already works this way (Turkish, Korean) skip them.

**Check.** The computed order is compared with the hand-ordered list; large disagreements are reviewed before use.
