# 009 — Curriculum order

Status: accepted

**One queue.** Words, grammar uses and, if chosen, kanji compete in one queue, ranked by how much real text each unlocks for this learner per unit of effort. A grammar use costs more effort than a word; the ratio is a setting. Unlock value is computed on de-duplicated text with each source's weight capped, so repetitive sources don't skew the order.

**Hard edges.** Kana first. A form after what it is derived from (007). A pattern after its parts, unless met as a chunk. A system lesson before its members.

**Building blocks** are ranked together with the cheapest thing that depends on them (007).

**Pace.** New items stop at the daily load limit (005). New grammar uses have their own daily cap (a setting).

**The graph.** Grammar items, their parts and edges are authored data, seeded from a hand-ordered beginner list with vocabulary moved to words and the grammar natural text needs added: てしまう, ようだ/みたい/らしい, はず, わけ, ため, ように, ところ, ばかり, honorific and humble verbs, and the structure items of 008.

**Placement.** Systems and structure items can be passed with a quick check, so learners whose first language already works this way (Turkish, Korean) skip them.

**Check.** The computed order is compared with the hand-ordered list; large disagreements are reviewed before use.
